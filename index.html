Conghao Li

LEO satellite networks · Onboard computing · AI service continuity

I am an undergraduate student in Internet of Things Engineering at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, enrolled in a dual-degree programme with Queen Mary University of London (expected graduation: June 2027).

My research began with traffic-aware constellation design, link allocation and handover in multi-layer LEO networks. I now also study onboard satellite-image compression and generative-AI service continuity in non-terrestrial edge–cloud systems, with an interest in how limited computing and communication resources shape service quality.

Education & Overview

B.E. in Internet of Things Engineering, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (International School), expected graduation June 2027.

Dual degree programme with Queen Mary University of London. Current GPA: 3.7/4.0.

Selected coursework includes Advanced Mathematics, Discrete Mathematics, Data Structures, Digital Circuit Design and Machine Learning. These courses provide a solid foundation for my interdisciplinary research combining communications and artificial intelligence.

Current Research

At CUHK-Shenzhen, I develop region-wise adaptive compression for satellite images, using reinforcement learning to balance transmission size and image distortion. At HKU's WILL Lab, I investigate mobility-aware state management for generative-AI services, including service placement and handover under changing satellite connectivity.

My work at BUPT provides the network-modelling foundation: STK–MATLAB/Python co-simulation, traffic-aware optimization and evaluation of dynamic LEO networks. Read more about my research experience →

Methods & Tools

Python and MATLAB for scientific computing; STK for orbital and link modelling; graph-based methods, clustering, multi-objective optimization and system-level simulation. I also use Git, Linux and LaTeX in my research workflow.
