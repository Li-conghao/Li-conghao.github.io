Research Experience

My work connects resource optimization in dynamic satellite networks with onboard image processing and the continuity of AI services.

Onboard Adaptive Compression of High-Resolution Satellite Images

Visiting Undergraduate Student · CUHK-Shenzhen · Jun. 2026–Present

Laboratory of Sensing and Communication for Smart Cities
Advisor: Assoc. Prof. Man-On Pun

Develop a task-agnostic compression agent that combines lightweight visual features and spatial context to select region-wise JPEG quality levels for satellite images. The goal is to reduce transmission size while controlling pixel distortion.

Built an xView/fMoW data pipeline with variable-size tiling and actual bitstream measurement, and implemented and tested a reinforcement-learning policy for adaptive compression.

Generative-AI Service Continuity in NTN Edge–Cloud Systems

Undergraduate Researcher · The University of Hong Kong · Jun. 2026–Present

WILL Lab
Advisor: Asst. Prof. Xianhao Chen

Investigate mobility-aware state management for generative-AI services in dynamic non-terrestrial edge–cloud systems. The service state includes conversational context, KV caches, retrieval results, tool states and session memory.

Model service-anchor placement and handover decisions to study the trade-offs among session continuity, state-migration overhead and response latency. This work is an ongoing investigation.

Dynamic Resource Optimization for Non-Terrestrial Networks

Undergraduate Researcher · BUPT · Oct. 2024–Present

Key Laboratory of Universal Wireless Communications
Advisors: Prof. Gang Chuai, Assoc. Prof. Weidong Gao and Assoc. Researcher Kaisa Zhang

Built STK–MATLAB/Python co-simulations of orbital visibility, dynamic topology, heterogeneous regional traffic, and satellite access and handover processes. Formulated traffic-aware constellation design and inter-layer link allocation with spatio-temporal graphs, surrogate-assisted multi-objective optimization and graph-informed marginal-gain selection.

Designed user-clustering and QoS-aware paging methods using sparse spectral clustering, LSTM-based traffic prediction and feedback-adaptive thresholds. Evaluated coverage, delay, blocking, energy consumption, load balance, resilience and signalling overhead.

Hyperspectral–LiDAR Fusion for Land-Cover Classification

Undergraduate Researcher · BUPT · Sep. 2025–Apr. 2026

State Key Laboratory of Networking and Switching Technology
Advisors: Assoc. Prof. Wenjia Xu and Prof. Mugen Peng

Contributed to a fusion network combining spectral–spatial feature extraction, structure-aware LiDAR tokens, LiDAR-guided channel-wise adaptation and Swin Transformer fusion.

Conducted benchmarking and ablation studies on Houston, Trento and MUUFL, comparing classification performance with CNN- and Transformer-based baselines. The resulting HLFN paper was accepted at IGARSS 2026.
